ingredients:
- 2 cups milk, warm
- 1 tbsp instant yeast
- 1 tsp sugar
- 5.5-6.5 cups flour
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tbsp salt

directions:
- Combine milk, sugar, and yeast in stand mixer at a low speed. Still for 5 minutes to allow the yeast to activate and get foamy.
- Add the melted butter, honey, egg, and salt.
- Slowly mix in 5.5 cups of flour using the dough hook attachment on a low speed.
- If needed, further add small amounts of flour until the dough is tacky but not sticky.
- Let the mixer knead the bread on low speed for 5 minutes.
- Scrape the dough into a ball, place into a large oiled bowl. Cover with a damp towel.
- Allow the dough to rise for ~1 hour, or until doubled in size. While waiting, grease two 9"x5" loaf pans.
- Punch down dough and turn out onto a lightly floured surface.
- Divide dough in half and shape each half into a log. Place one in each loaf pan.
- Cover the loaves with a damp towel and let rise again for 30-45 minutes, or until doubled in size.
- Preheat the oven to 350°F.
- Bake the leaves for 30-35 minutes, until golden brrown on top and hollow-sounding when tapped.
- Allow to cool on a wire rack for at least 1 hour before slicing.
