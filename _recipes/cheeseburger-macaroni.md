ingredients:
- 2 lbs ground beef
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 4 cups beef stock
- 10.5 oz can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 16 oz elbow macaroni, uncooked
- 6-8 oz colby jack cheese, small pieces
- 4-6 oz pepperjack cheese, small pieces

directions:
- Start a large skillet over medium-high heat
- Add ground beef and break it up into grumbles. Cook until there is no pink left, about 10 minutes.
- Drain excess grease. Add ground beef to the bottom of a slow cooker
- Add the onion, beef stock, cream of mushroom soup (non-diluted), and seasonings. Stir to combine
- Allow slow cooker to simmer on low heat, covered, for 4-6 hours on low, or 2-4 hours on high. Stir every hour, if possible.
- Add uncooked elbow macaroni and stir to combine.
- Continue to cook for 20-30 minutes, stirrirng every 10 minutes until the noodles are al dente.
- Add the cheese and cover until melted, then stir in.
- Serve.
